# Funding and community support

INNERnet is free and open-source under Apache-2.0; no payments, donations, account creation or marketplace membership are required to implement or self-host the protocol.

Optional donations, grants, sponsorships and services may support the project. No donation or sponsorship should confer special control over the open protocol or its security decisions.

**Donations have not been activated yet.** We will not publish a payment link until a legal receiving entity or eligible fiscal host and verified checkout are established. The website will update when the payment route is operational.

Project website: https://innernetcorp.com/support.html
