    Go to your dashboard

    Your machine

    What you offer

    Bandwidth

    Your internet connection, by the GiB. Check that your internet plan allows it.

    Compute

    Clients bring their own programs and models; you rent out the machine's power, by the hour.

    When you offer it

    In this machine's time zone (local). Clients only use it inside these hours, and uptime is counted only inside them.

    DayFromTo

    A window that ends earlier than it starts runs past midnight (for example 21:00 to 08:00). 00:00 to 24:00 is the whole day.

    You offer storage with limited hours. Clients cannot fetch their files while this machine is off, so they will prefer machines that are always on. Consider offering storage only on an always-on machine.

    Your payout contract

    Clients pay this contract. It sends 97.5% to your wallet and 2.5% to the network, and nobody can change that.

    Connect the wallet that should receive your share: a wallet on Base that you control, such as the Coinbase Wallet app, MetaMask, Rabby or a hardware wallet. Do not use an exchange deposit address. You never give anyone its key; the contract only stores its address. The same address works on the test network and later on Base mainnet.

    Finish on your machine

    Your browser locks the passphrase with a key only your machine has, so this website only passes on scrambled text. The machine writes the encrypted backup to ~/.innernet/provider-backup.json; copy that file somewhere safe and keep the passphrase.

    To back up the key from here, update the provider on your machine. For now run innernet-provider backup FILE on it.

    Apply asks for your passkey. Your machine checks that your passkey confirmed exactly this, signs it with its own key and applies it. You can follow every step here.
