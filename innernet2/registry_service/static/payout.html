Your payout contract

Clients pay this contract. It sends 97.5% to your wallet and 2.5% to the INNERnet network. It has no owner, so nobody, including INNERnet, can change where the money goes.

1. Connect your wallet

Use a browser wallet such as MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet or Trust Wallet. You pay a small network fee in test ETH. You can get it free from a Base Sepolia faucet.

2. Create the contract

This is usually the wallet you just connected. It can be any wallet you control on Base Sepolia. It cannot be changed later.
