Your payout contract

Clients pay this contract. It sends 97.5% to your wallet and 2.5% to the INNERNET network. It has no owner, so nobody, including INNERNET, can change where the money goes.

1. Connect a wallet

Creating your payout contract is one transaction, made once. It costs a network fee of about $0.01, paid in ETH on Base by the wallet you connect, so that wallet needs a little ETH on Base. You can buy it in your wallet app, or withdraw it from an exchange and choose the Base network. Collecting payments later costs about the same.

Scan this with your wallet app (Trust Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Binance Wallet, Rainbow and others), then approve the connection there. On this phone? Open your wallet app.

2. Create the contract

This is usually the wallet you just connected. It can be any wallet you control on Base; do not use an exchange deposit address. It cannot be changed later. Your wallet app then asks you to approve the transaction.
